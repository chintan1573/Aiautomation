# Storyboard — Peanut Butter Protein Cheesecake Bites

Format: vertical 9:16 · 5 shots · one continuous voiceover.

## Shot 1 — The Hook

**Omni Flash prompt**

> Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio. A realistic close-up food vlog shot of a finished peanut butter protein cheesecake bite centered on a small white ceramic plate in a real home kitchen. A fork presses gently into the middle and cuts through the creamy cheesecake layer, revealing the soft interior and crumbly biscuit base underneath. A thin ribbon of natural peanut butter drips slowly over the top and settles onto the cheesecake. Bright natural kitchen window light, realistic food textures, shallow depth of field, subtle handheld camera movement, highly appetizing but completely natural and photorealistic. Keep the dessert and fork centered safely within the frame.

**Voiceover**

> Want cheesecake with a serious protein upgrade? These peanut butter cheesecake bites are ridiculously easy.

## Shot 2 — The Assembly

**Omni Flash prompt**

> Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio. A tight top-down vertical kitchen shot showing hands preparing peanut butter protein cheesecake bites in the center of the frame. Crushed digestive biscuits are mixed with a small amount of melted butter in a bowl, then pressed firmly into the bottom of small silicone muffin cups. In a second bowl, thick Greek yogurt, cream cheese, vanilla protein powder, and peanut butter are stirred together until smooth and creamy. The spoon visibly leaves thick trails through the mixture. Everything happens naturally on a clean kitchen countertop under bright daylight, with realistic hand movement and authentic food textures. Keep all important ingredients and actions centered for mobile framing.

**Voiceover**

> Make a quick biscuit base, then mix Greek yogurt, protein powder, cream cheese, and peanut butter until smooth.

## Shot 3 — The Sizzle / Setting Action

**Omni Flash prompt**

> Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio. A realistic macro food-preparation shot in a home kitchen. The thick peanut butter cheesecake mixture is spooned generously into the center of each prepared muffin cup and gently smoothed with the back of a spoon. The mixture settles naturally into the cups without spilling. A small drizzle of peanut butter is added across the surface and lightly swirled with a toothpick, creating natural marbled patterns. The camera moves slightly closer with subtle handheld movement. Bright natural overhead and window lighting, shallow depth of field, realistic creamy texture and real-world food physics. Keep the muffin cups centered and fully visible.

**Voiceover**

> Fill the cups, add a little peanut butter on top, then give it a quick swirl. That’s it.

**Omni Flash prompt**
